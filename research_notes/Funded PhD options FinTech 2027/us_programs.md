# Funded US PhD Programs in FinTech-Adjacent Fields (Finance / IS-MIS / Analytics / Accounting), Fall 2027 Entry

Method note: the egress proxy blocked WebFetch to every .edu domain tried (robinson.gsu.edu, jindal.utdallas.edu, fisher.osu.edu). So all figures below come from **search-engine snippets of official .edu pages**, and I could not read the pages in full. Each figure is cited to the official URL the snippet came from. Anything a snippet attributes to an earlier cohort (e.g., "Fall 2024", "Fall 2025 admits", "fall 2026") is flagged. Please re-check every number on the live page before relying on it.

## Q1. Program list with key facts (deadline, fee, stipend, funding years, tests, English test)

### Takeaway
I found about 17 US business-school PhD programs with published stipends of at least $23k in Finance, IS/MIS, ISOM or Business Analytics. Most mid-ranked public programs pay $25k–$47k and fund 5 years. Almost all of them **require** GMAT/GRE. Application fees run $30–$100, and fee waivers for international applicants are rare (explicitly none at GSU and Arizona).

### Cited Findings

## Q2. Which programs are GRE/GMAT optional or waivable?

### Takeaway
Among the mid-ranked business PhDs checked, only **University at Buffalo** is explicitly test-optional. GSU, Houston, Oklahoma State, KU, FSU, Arizona, Temple, UTD, Minnesota and Arkansas all explicitly require GMAT/GRE, and GSU and Houston say it cannot be waived. The applicant should plan on taking the GRE.

### Cited Findings
- UB School of Management PhD: "not required to take the GMAT or GRE" — [UB PhD admissions](https://management.buffalo.edu/degree-programs/phd-in-management/admissions.html)
- GSU Robinson: test score waivers "are not granted" — [Robinson PhD application process](https://robinson.gsu.edu/phd/application-process/). Note that GSU's 2021 removal of GMAT/GRE applied to **master's** programs, not the PhD — [GSU News 2021](https://news.gsu.edu/2021/10/22/georgia-state-universitys-robinson-college-of-business-eliminates-gmat-gre-requirement/)
- UH Bauer: "cannot be waived. There are no exceptions" — [Bauer doctoral application process](https://www.bauer.uh.edu/doctoral/admissions/application-process.php)
- Arizona Eller MIS: required, but **no minimum score** — [Eller MIS PhD admissions](https://eller.arizona.edu/programs/doctoral/mis/admissions)
- Posted test benchmarks: GSU avg GRE 322; OSU-Stillwater min GRE 320 (avg 323); FSU ≥155 per section; USF Finance/IS GRE above the 90th percentile; UTD GMAT ≥600 or equivalent — sources as cited in Q1.
- Test-optional business PhDs also appear on generic aggregator lists (e.g., [ResearchTweet/Booth](https://researchtweet.com/academic-jobs/fully-funded-phd-in-business-at-university-of-chicago-booth/), [BU Questrom admissions](https://www.bu.edu/questrom/degree-programs/phd-program/admissions)), but the search result did not confirm test-optional status for Questrom.

### Inferences
- A GRE of **~320–325 (Quant 163–167+)** is the realistic target to be competitive at the target-tier programs above. Finance programs weight Quant most heavily.
- Some iSchool Information Science PhDs (non-business) waive the GRE. Examples: Arizona iSchool ("GRE not required") [UA InfoSci](https://infosci.arizona.edu/phd-information/admissions-funding), and Illinois iSchool (exempt with GPA ≥3.0) [Illinois iSchool](https://ischool.illinois.edu/academics/graduate/phd-information-sciences/apply). They are a possible no-GRE fallback, but they are less FinTech-focused.

### Gaps
- I did not confirm the current test policy for Purdue, Pitt, Iowa, UNL, UF or Ohio State PhDs.

## Q3. Realistic reach / target / safer tiers for this profile

### Takeaway
The profile's strengths are a very high CGPA (9.475/10, top of final year), a Warwick MSc FinTech, three papers, and Python/SQL/R with ML projects. Its weaknesses are a non-elite Indian undergrad institution, papers in non-top venues, and no GRE yet. Together this fits **IS/MIS and business-analytics PhDs at mid-ranked public R1s** best. Finance PhDs, even mid-ranked ones, weight GRE Quant and math preparation more heavily and are the harder path.

### Cited Findings
- Cohorts are tiny: GSU admits 1–4 per department per year [Robinson PhD FAQs](https://robinson.gsu.edu/phd/faqs/), and UF ISOM admits 2–3 per year [UF ISOM PhD](https://warrington.ufl.edu/graduate/phd-information-systems-operations-management/). Every program is individually competitive.
- Posted admit test profiles (GSU avg GMAT 710 / GRE 322; OSU MSIS avg GRE 323) signal the bar at "target"-tier schools — [Robinson PhD application process](https://robinson.gsu.edu/phd/application-process/); [OSU MIS PhD](https://business.okstate.edu/watson/msis-phd/index.html)
- UTD admission criteria explicitly weigh GPA, GMAT/GRE, references, **business and professional experience**, a statement of objectives and **fit with faculty research** — [Jindal PhD admission requirements](https://jindal.utdallas.edu/phd-programs/admission-requirements/)

### Inferences (not sourced; judgement only)
- **Reach:** Chicago Booth, Minnesota Carlson, Purdue, Ohio State Fisher, UT Dallas (strong in IS/Finance research), UF ISOM, Pitt Katz, Arizona Eller MIS (a top-5 MIS research program).
- **Target:** GSU Robinson CIS (a large IS department with fintech/blockchain faculty), USF Muma (IS or Finance), FSU (MIS), Temple Fox MIS, Houston Bauer (DISC/MIS), KU (Business: IS/Analytics or Finance), Iowa Tippie Business Analytics, UB Management (MIS/Finance; test-optional).
- **Safer (relatively):** Oklahoma State MSIS, Arkansas Walton IS, Nebraska-Lincoln. They have lower but acceptable stipends ($24–30k) and posted minimums (e.g., OSU GRE 320) that a well-prepared applicant can clear.
- A BBA plus a specialised master's is common among admits at IS/MIS programs. At Finance programs, admits more often have economics/math/engineering backgrounds. No source quantified this (see Gaps).
- The highest-leverage actions: take the GRE (aim for Quant ≥165), name 2–3 faculty per school whose fintech/payments/blockchain/fraud-ML work matches the applicant's papers and projects, and get a research letter from a Warwick supervisor.
- UK Student visa note: applying from the UK is fine. The F-1 is processed via a US consulate, typically in the country of residence or India (not researched here).

### Gaps
- No official acceptance rates were found for any program. I did not run Grad Cafe or Reddit searches, given the tool budget.
- No source quantified the share of admits with a BBA-only undergrad or a master's degree.
