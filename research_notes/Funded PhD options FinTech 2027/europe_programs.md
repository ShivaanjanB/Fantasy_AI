## Q1. What are the options? Institution, window, fee, salary, tuition, duration, tests, English

### Takeaway
At least 20 funded routes exist. Dutch, Nordic, Swiss and Luxembourg positions are **employment contracts** paying roughly €33k–€60k gross. Mannheim (year 1) and ESADE pay stipends **below** the €21k threshold. Application fees are €0 at most places. Confirmed exceptions are **INSEAD (€80)** and **Swiss Finance Institute (USD 100)**. Bocconi's PhD application is reported as free. I could not confirm HEC Paris's fee. Most structured programs have **January (or earlier) 2027 deadlines**. Rolling individual positions (Luxembourg, Basel, Dutch/Nordic project vacancies) are the only things "open now".

## Q2. Which options are realistic vs reach for this applicant?

### Takeaway
The applicant is an Indian national with a Warwick MSc FinTech, a strong BBA GPA, non-top-tier papers and no GRE/GMAT. They are **most competitive for individually advertised project PhDs** in FinTech, DeFi or AI-in-finance, where a PI hires on skill fit (Python/ML, fraud/credit, blockchain). Examples are Luxembourg, Basel, Dutch/Nordic project vacancies and CBS/BI calls. Econ-style finance programs (Tinbergen, SFI/EPFL, Bocconi, HEC, INSEAD, SSE, Mannheim, ERIM finance) are **reaches** without a strong GRE Q and a research master. **Taking the GRE (target Q ≥ 165) before January 2027 is the single highest-leverage step**, because Tilburg and EPFL require it for this profile and ESE sets a hard Q-160 cut.

### Cited Findings
- Tilburg requires GRE/GMAT from holders of non-EU degrees — [Tilburg admission](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/research/economics-and-management/graduate-school/phd-business/admission-and-application).
- Erasmus ESE: GRE required, and Q below 160 is not considered — [ESE requirements](https://www.eur.nl/en/ese/working/phd-vacancies/admission-requirements). ERIM allows applications with no GMAT/GRE if other evidence of excellence is supplied — [ERIM admission](https://www.eur.nl/en/ese/research/phd/phd-programme/erasmus-phd-programmes-management-and-economics/full-time/admission).
- EPFL requires GMAT/GRE, and UZH requires it from degree holders outside the Bologna area — [SFI FAQ](https://www.sfi.ch/en/phd/faq).
- SSE admits at most 4 finance PhDs per year — [SSE](https://www.hhs.se/en/education/phd/finance/admission/).
- Luxembourg explicitly encourages quantitative/CS backgrounds — [uni.lu](https://www.uni.lu/en/jobs/doctoral-researcher-positions-in-finance/).
- Basel's DeFi/blockchain PhD is a single rolling-hire project post — [unibas](https://careers.unibas.ch/job/Basel-PhD-position-in-Decentralized-Finance-and-Blockchain-based-Financial-Market-Infrastructure-Pete/1325811957/).
- MSCA DN posts require 300 ECTS with ≥60 at master's level. A 3-year Indian BBA (≈180 ECTS) plus a 1-year UK MSc (≈90 ECTS) may fall **short of 300** — [EURAXESS 90339](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs/90339).
