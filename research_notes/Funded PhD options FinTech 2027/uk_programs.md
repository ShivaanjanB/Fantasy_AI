## Q0. Baseline: current UKRI stipend rate and London weighting

### Takeaway
UKRI minimum stipend for 2026/27 (from 1 Oct 2026) is **£21,805** outside London and **£23,805** in London (+£2,000 London weighting), up 4.9% from £20,780 / £22,780 in 2025/26. A 2027/28 rate had not been found as of Sept 2026. So "≥ £23k" in practice means (a) a London UKRI-rate award, or (b) a business-school award paying above UKRI.

### Cited Findings
- 2026/27 UKRI minimum is £21,805 (up from £20,780), and £23,805 with London weighting (up from £22,780). Effective 1 Oct 2026. Tax-free — [Research Professional News](https://www.researchprofessionalnews.com/rr-news-uk-careers-2026-2-ukri-increases-phd-stipend-by-4-9-per-cent-for-2026-27/); [AcademicJobs (aggregator)](https://www.academicjobs.com/higher-education-news/uk-phd-stipends-rise-2026-ukri-49-to-21805-academicjobs-3570)
- UKRI studentships fund only up to the home fee level. International students may have to make up the difference — [UKRI support for funded students](https://www.ukri.org/manage-your-award/support-for-ukri-funded-students/); [Cardiff UKRI international page](https://www.cardiff.ac.uk/study/international/funding-and-fees/international-scholarships/ukri-international)
- ESRC DTPs cap international studentships at **30%** of awards each year. ESRC pays the home fee only, and the home/international fee gap (quoted as £16,512–£19,212/yr for 2026/27) must be covered by the institution or DTP. Some partners waive it and some cover only a limited number — [NINE DTP eligibility](https://www.ninedtp.ac.uk/eligibility/); [SWDTP eligibility](https://www.swdtp.ac.uk/prospective-students-and-fellows/are-you-eligible-for-esrc-studentship-funding/); [Warwick Midlands Graduate School DTP eligibility](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/cross_fac/mgsdtp/studentships/eligibility)

### Inferences
- Any studentship quoting "UKRI rate" outside London for a 2027 start will pay at least £21,805, and slightly more if UKRI uplifts again for 2027/28. That sits in the user's "£20,780–£23,000 flagged" band, not ≥ £23k.
- A 2027/28 UKRI rate is typically announced in early 2027. It is unknown today.

### Gaps
- The 2027/28 UKRI rate is not yet published (no source found).

## Q2. Realistic vs reach for this profile; MSc grade required

### Takeaway
The profile is strong on grades (9.475/10 BBA, topped the year) and FinTech/ML skills, but weak on quantitative-economics training, GRE/GMAT and top-venue research. **Finance PhD tracks** (Imperial, UCL Fin Econ, LSE Finance, Oxford, Cambridge, WBS F&E) are **reach**: they expect heavy math/econometrics and GRE/GMAT. **Information Systems / Analytics / Accounting / FinTech-applied tracks** (WBS ISM & B&M, AMBS, Durham, Edinburgh, Lancaster, Bath, KCL, AI CDTs) are **more realistic**, especially **Warwick**, where he is an internal MSc student who can secure a supervisor and referees locally.

### Cited Findings
- UCL SoM requires a minimum of a first-class bachelor's (or equivalent) plus GMAT/GRE — [UCL SoM PhD](https://www.mgmt.ucl.ac.uk/phd)
- WBS MRes/PhD requires GRE/GMAT as a formal requirement with no threshold — [WBS Finance PhD applying](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/soc/wbs/subjects/finance/phd/applying/)
- Oxford Saïd DPhil Finance requires official GRE scores — [Saïd DPhil Finance](https://www.sbs.ox.ac.uk/programmes/degrees/dphil-finance)
- Cambridge Judge does not require GRE/GMAT, and a score "will not add any value" — [CJBS MPhil Finance FAQs](https://www.jbs.cam.ac.uk/phd-research-masters/mphil-finance/faqs/)
- Warwick Chancellor's International Scholarship targets "outstanding international PhD applicants" classified as overseas — [Warwick DC competitions](https://warwick.ac.uk/services/dc/schols_fund/applicants/competitions/)

### Inferences (no single source; judgement)
- **Realistic / good fit:** WBS MRes/PhD B&M (Information Systems & Management or Accounting group, with GRE/GMAT taken by Dec), Warwick Chancellor's Intl (through a WBS/Statistics/CS supervisor), AMBS studentship (Accounting & Finance or Info/Analytics), Durham DUBS, Edinburgh EDC, Lancaster LUMS, Bath GRS, KCL Business School, Midlands Graduate School ESRC DTP (Warwick-led, but only 30% of places go to international applicants).
- **Reach:** Imperial Business School (Finance), UCL SoM Financial Economics, LSE Finance, Oxford DPhil Finance, Cambridge Judge (MPhil Finance route).
- **Competitive but plausible:** UKRI AI CDTs (Bath, Bristol). They want CS/maths-heavy backgrounds, and his ML projects help but the BBA is a weakness.
- **MSc grade:** Most UK business-school PhDs expect a **Distinction (70%+) or strong Merit (65%+)** MSc and a first-class-equivalent UG. Competitive fully funded awards (Chancellor's, Imperial, UCL, LSE) effectively need **Distinction-level** predicted marks and a strong proposal. *(No official source captured states the numeric threshold; treat as a general expectation.)*
- **Internal-applicant advantage at Warwick:** He can meet WBS faculty in person to co-develop a proposal and get referees who are WBS academics. Warwick PGR scholarship competitions (Chancellor's) require departmental nomination, so a supervisor who champions him is decisive. *(Inference; Warwick nomination mechanics not verified on page.)*
- **GRE/GMAT:** Taking the GRE (quant ≥165) in Oct–Nov 2026 would unlock WBS, UCL, Imperial and Oxford, which all require it. Cambridge does not.

### Gaps
- Official numeric MSc grade thresholds were not captured for any programme.
- Admission statistics or success rates for international applicants were not found.
