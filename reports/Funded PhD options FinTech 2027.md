# Target IS-leaning PhDs before October closes

As of 26 September 2026, this research found about **80 funded PhD routes** in Finance, FinTech, Information Systems, Business Analytics and Accounting across the UK, US, Canada, continental Europe and Asia-Pacific. For the 2027 entry cycle, **about 16 are confirmed open now or hiring on a rolling basis**, including Georgia State, Rotman, Cambridge Judge, HKPFS, HKU, HKBU, CUHK, NUS, SMU, UNSW, UTS, UQ, Monash, Queen's Smith, Luxembourg and Basel. About **6 more almost certainly open** because their deadlines fall in October–December 2026 (UBC Sauder, UCL, AMBS, Oxford Saïd, Melbourne and SMU SCIS), but no source confirmed it. At least **6 are confirmed to open later**: NTU on 1 Oct, Tinbergen around 1 Oct, Ivey on 15 Oct, Warwick Chancellor's in October, Mannheim in mid-November and SSE on 1 Dec. Most other US and European programs have December–January deadlines and portals whose opening dates were not captured. Application fees are low almost everywhere except North America. UK applications cost **£0–£20** (Oxford and Cambridge charge £20). Most continental European ones are **€0**, with **INSEAD at €80 and the Swiss Finance Institute at USD 100**. US business PhDs charge **USD 30–100**, Canadian schools **CAD 130–168 (about USD 94–121)**, Hong Kong **HK$180–780 (about USD 23–100)** and NUS **S$50**. Fees for Australian research degrees were not verified. Roughly 55 of these routes pay at least USD 23k. Measured in dollars, every UK studentship at the UKRI rate (£21,805, about USD 29k) clears the bar. Only **Imperial (£29,580), UCL (£26,000), the UKRI AI CDTs (~£25k), King's and London ESRC DTPs (~£23,805) and WBS Business & Management (~£23k, unverified)** clear it in pounds. For this applicant, the best balance of odds and pay lies in **Information Systems and analytics tracks rather than Finance**. The main places are Warwick itself, CityU and HKBU in Hong Kong, the non-Go8 end of Australia, and mid-ranked US public universities (Buffalo, Oklahoma State, Arkansas, Georgia State, Florida State). **Taking the GRE by mid-November 2026 is the single step that unlocks most of the list.**

**How reliable these numbers are.** In every regional search, the network proxy blocked direct access to official university pages. **Every figure below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from full page reads.** Some extracts came from aggregators, and many carry figures from the 2025 or 2026 cycle. Such figures are flagged "older" or "prior cycle" throughout. Check every number on the official page before you pay a fee or plan around a deadline. The Safer / Target / Reach tiers are **my judgement from posted requirements, test benchmarks and cohort sizes. They are not admission statistics.** None of the programs published acceptance rates, and the only cohort sizes found were Georgia State (1–4 per department per year) and UF ISOM (2–3 per year).

## GRE timing, Warwick access and IS fit drive the odds

**Test first.** Without a GRE the applicant can realistically reach only Buffalo, CityU, Cambridge Judge, ERIM, Luxembourg, the Australian universities and the UK schools that do not require one. The following programs require GMAT/GRE and several refuse to waive it:

- **Refuse waivers:** Georgia State and Houston ([Robinson](https://robinson.gsu.edu/phd/application-process/); [Bauer](https://www.bauer.uh.edu/doctoral/admissions/application-process.php)); Ivey ([Ivey](https://www.ivey.uwo.ca/phd/admissions/apply/)); Queen's, except for Canadian degrees ([Smith](https://smith.queensu.ca/grad_studies/PHD/program-details/admissions.php)).
- **Mandatory:** HKU, CUHK and SMU LKCSB (see master table).
- **Required with a formal requirement but no threshold:** WBS ([Warwick Finance PhD](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/soc/wbs/subjects/finance/phd/applying/)).
- **Required for non-EU degrees:** Tilburg ([Tilburg](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/research/economics-and-management/graduate-school/phd-business/admission-and-application)).
- **Hard cut:** Erasmus School of Economics does not consider applicants with GRE Quant below 160 ([ESE](https://www.eur.nl/en/ese/working/phd-vacancies/admission-requirements)).

The benchmarks give a working target of **about 320–325 overall with Quant ≥165**. Georgia State admits averaged GRE 322, Oklahoma State sets a 320 minimum, and FSU asks for ≥155 per section ([OSU](https://business.okstate.edu/watson/msis-phd/index.html); [FSU](https://business.fsu.edu/phd)). The 165 Quant figure is my judgement, not a posted cutoff. Queen's closes on 31 October, so only an early-October sitting helps there. For the 1 December cluster, **scores are needed by mid-November**. Sit the test at a **test centre**: NTU rejects at-home GRE/GMAT scores taken after 2023 ([NBS](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/business/admissions/phd-programme/admission/application)), although UT Dallas accepts online scores for Fall 2027.

**Use Warwick's internal advantage deliberately.** WBS states that its MRes/PhD "fully funds all successful applicants" ([WBS fees](https://www.wbs.ac.uk/courses/doctoral/mres-phd-business-management/fees/)). The Chancellor's International competition is aimed at "outstanding international PhD applicants" and opens its call in October ([Warwick DC](https://warwick.ac.uk/services/dc/schols_fund/applicants/competitions/)). Being on campus lets the applicant co-develop a proposal with a WBS Information Systems & Management or Accounting academic and get research-active Warwick referees. That supervisor's backing probably decides a departmental nomination, though the nomination mechanics were not verified on the page. The same Warwick letters strengthen every other application, especially in Hong Kong, Singapore and Australia, where committees know Jain University less well. The weakest point in the file is timing. The Warwick MSc result will not be final until late 2026, so most applications will rest on **predicted or interim marks**. The UK notes suggest that competitive awards effectively expect Distinction-level performance, but no official source gave a threshold.

**Write one proposal and adapt it.** Research proposals matter most in the UK, Australia, Luxembourg and project hires like Basel. Payments security, fraud-detection ML and blockchain payment infrastructure fit IS, FinTech and DeFi groups far better than the taxation paper does. For Australia, ask Warwick for a letter confirming the dissertation's credit weight. Monash admits coursework master's graduates only if research makes up at least **25% of a full-time year**, with an honours-equivalent average of 65+ ([Monash](https://www.monash.edu/graduate-research/study/apply/admission-criteria)). A 60-credit Warwick dissertation should clear that, but other Go8 universities may demand more. Contact supervisors before applying.

**Choose IS over Finance where the fields overlap.** Finance PhDs at every tier draw economics, maths and engineering applicants with high Quant scores and look for graduate econometrics. IS and MIS programs routinely admit BBA holders with a specialised master's and value the Python, SQL, R and ML project work this applicant has. This comes from the regional analyses, not from admissions data. A Finance application still makes sense at WBS, ERIM and Luxembourg, where the fit is FinTech-specific. Three smaller risks should be checked:

- **English tests:** UT Dallas waives TOEFL/IELTS only by the applicant's native country, so an Indian national still needs a test there ([Jindal](https://jindal.utdallas.edu/phd-programs/admission-requirements/)). Whether a Warwick MSc counts as an exemption elsewhere was not confirmed.
- **Degree length:** MSCA Doctoral Networks require 300 ECTS ([EURAXESS](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs/90339)), which a 3-year BBA plus a 1-year MSc may not reach.
- **Visa and permit questions:** a new UK CAS, a US F-1 and a Quebec CAQ were not researched.

## Conclusion

The money is not the constraint. Once converted to dollars, almost every tuition-covering business PhD in Hong Kong, Singapore, Australia, northern Europe and the mid-ranked US public universities clears USD 23k. So does every UK UKRI-rate studentship. The £23k line narrows the UK to London and a few above-UKRI awards, and in the UK the internal Warwick route beats most of them on odds. **Eligibility and timing are the real constraints.** A GRE taken by mid-November roughly doubles the set of programs worth applying to. The October Australian deadlines (UTS 7 Oct, UQ 19 Oct, UNSW 30 Oct) are the only chance to act before a score exists.

The best way to use this list is as a portfolio. File the three Australian applications and line up a Warwick supervisor in October. Sit the GRE in early November. Submit the 1–15 December cluster: HKPFS/CityU, AMBS, Chancellor's, Buffalo, Oklahoma State, Arkansas and FSU. Finish with WBS, Georgia State and the March fallbacks (UQ Round 2, Monash). Before paying any fee, reopen each official page: every figure here came through search extracts rather than the pages themselves.
